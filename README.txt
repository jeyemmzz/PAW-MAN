PAW-MAN: BERRY CHASE

A retro arcade maze game with a furry twist! Instead of Pac-Man, you play as
a dog. Race through the neon maze, eat every dot and cherry, and dodge the
chasing Pac-Man. Grab a Big Dot to turn the tables: your dog can hit Pac-Man
and knock him out for 10 seconds!

Choose your dog: Molly, Buddy, Archer or Yoda.

Play on PC with W A S D or the arrow keys, or on phone and tablet with the
on-screen joystick.

A fan-made tribute, not affiliated with Bandai Namco.
